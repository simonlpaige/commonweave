# From framework to a testable service

Proposal for review, 2026-09-05. This supplies an operational test of the framework; it does not enact governance changes, create a legal entity, assign volunteers, or establish partnerships. Existing [governance](GOVERNANCE.md), [critique](CRITIQUE.md), [claim ledger](CLAIMS.md), and [falsifiers](FALSIFIERS.md) remain visible.

**Working proposition:** people solving a defined local problem may save time by finding complementary organizations, understanding why each is relevant, and seeing what is missing. Test that proposition before interpreting a larger directory as a stronger movement. It can be useful whether automation accelerates, stalls, or concentrates power.

## Five distinctions that the product must preserve

| Distinction | Practical rule |
|---|---|
| Values versus evidence | Universal sufficiency and democratic participation are commitments. Listing an organization does not demonstrate that it realizes them. |
| Organization versus service | A registry record establishes a candidate entity. Service area, eligibility, cost, opening hours, capacity, and referral route each need their own evidence. |
| Place versus reach | An office pin is not a service boundary. Remote tools, national federations, and local providers need different treatment. |
| Hypothesis versus relationship | Proximity and complementary roles suggest a possibility. Only a dated, attributable source supports a reported relationship; current collaboration still needs confirmation. |
| Discovery versus outcome | A useful shortlist, an agreed introduction, and a completed service are separate outcomes. Never count map clicks as people helped. |

## Start with one problem in one place

Use a non-urgent organizer task, such as developing a community food project or researching a shared-equity housing pathway. Select the locality only after the audit finds at least three plausible complementary roles and a willing local reviewer. Do not promise a housing, healthcare, or crisis-referral service from this dataset.

The first pilot asks ten consenting practitioners to complete a realistic discovery task. Compare their usual method (search, existing directory, or personal network) with Commonweave, alternate order, and use comparable tasks to reduce learning effects. Record prior local knowledge and make participation possible without a GitHub account. Ten participants provide early design evidence, not a population-level estimate.

### Pilot card required before recruitment

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Problem and affected people | One sentence, geographic scope, who helped define it, and whose needs may be absent. |
| Baseline and comparator | Existing way to find help; time limit; what qualifies as a useful lead; scoring fixed before sessions. |
| Roles and dependencies | E.g. resident organizer → land steward → development capacity → finance; mark unfilled roles and enabling policy. |
| Evidence | Record IDs, original sources, dates, role/service-area evidence, and what remains unverified. |
| Ownership | Named consenting pilot lead, local reviewer, and separate result reviewer. No willing owner means no launch. |
| Resources | Staff/volunteer hours, cash ceiling, donated inputs, and who bears recurring work or exclusion costs. |
| Safety and exit | Public organizational facts only; no beneficiary case details; no forced referrals; withdraw or narrow the pilot when a participant or organization requests it. |
| Decision date | Day 30: continue, revise, or stop, with unsuccessful cases published in aggregate. |

## Measurable tests and decisions

These thresholds are proposed decision rules to be fixed on the pilot card before recruiting. They are not achieved results.

| Test | Day-30 gate | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery utility (operationalizes F-PROJ-4) | At least 7/10 practitioners find one independently checked useful lead within ten minutes. Record all ten outcomes. | Narrow coverage or redesign discovery; stop describing the map as a demonstrated coordination tool. |
| Added value | Commonweave yields lower median task time at comparable usefulness, or a useful lead missed by the comparator for at least 3/10 participants. | Improve the existing directory through contributions or links instead of claiming a new layer adds value. |
| Relationship comprehension (clarifies F-PROJ-1) | At least 9/10 distinguish suggested complementarity from a sourced relationship in a blind example. | Remove or simplify connection lines before inviting more users. Do not test inferred suggestions as if they claimed existing partnerships. |
| Useful suggestions | A local reviewer accepts at least 70% of 20 sampled role assignments as plausible, citing scope and constraints. | Suppress weak role assignments and repair classifier evidence; geographic closeness cannot rescue them. |
| Participation | Four reviewed, authorized artifact deliveries produce at least one substantive correction or use report. An open/click/automatic reply does not qualify. | Inspect delivery and audience fit, revise the offer, and pause additional outreach. Four silences cannot establish the cause. |
| Retention | At least three of the ten practitioners voluntarily reuse a corrected brief/map or make another correction within 30 days. | Fix practical value and maintenance before adding growth mechanisms. |
| Data stewardship | Every pilot-facing record has a source and review date; every proposed relationship shows its evidence state; every correction has a decision. | Hold that brief from distribution until the missing evidence is repaired. |

An introduction counts only when both sides voluntarily agree to it. A meeting counts only when confirmed by a participant. A real-world pilot outcome belongs to the implementing organizations; Commonweave should describe its contribution and alternative explanations without claiming credit for the whole result.

## Accountability at the current scale

Proposed operational roles can initially be small, but should be explicit. Simon Paige is the repository's named author; the active roster, actual access rights, and accepted responsibilities need confirmation before publishing a staffed service commitment.

| Responsibility | Proposed accountable role | Minimum record |
|---|---|---|
| Release and rollback | Repository maintainer | Release inputs, audit results, known limitations, prior export to restore. |
| Record and relationship correction | Data steward; second reviewer for contested or sensitive decisions | Before/after, sources, reason, reviewer, date; personal details excluded from public notes. |
| Pilot usefulness | Consenting local practitioner | Accepted/rejected leads and missing roles, with no beneficiary identities. |
| Appeals and conflicts | Reviewer uninvolved in disputed decision where available | Original decision, objection, outcome, and disclosed conflict. If none is available, hold the disputed promotion pending review. |
| Partnership or money | Existing governance process | Scope, permission, term, funding conditions, and public decision; a listing claim is neither membership nor endorsement. |

Proposed service target: acknowledge ordinary corrections within seven days and decide or give a reason within fourteen. Publish it as a commitment only after an owner accepts it. Urgent exposure/removal reports take priority; withdraw the affected public detail while reviewing credible risk. Log only a minimal public outcome. Organization self-attestation verifies authorship or particular facts, not independent quality, alignment, or an automatic Tier A upgrade.

## Economics and continuity

Measure maintenance before adding infrastructure. Proposed first-month ceiling: **24 maintainer hours** (8 evidence repair, 6 usability sessions, 4 artifact preparation, 4 corrections, 2 reporting). Cash is uncommitted; use existing hosting and keep any new spend optional pending a real budget. Reimburse or pay practitioner/translation work only with an agreed funded arrangement; do not imply funding exists.

Report cash costs, donated hours, oldest undecided correction, and hours per accepted correction. If work exceeds the time ceiling or the queue exceeds two weeks of available capacity, pause new ingest/outreach and reduce pilot scope. Unpaid effort is a cost, not free capacity.

Keep open discovery and corrections available regardless of payment. Later funding could support maintenance, training, or source integration, but should not purchase ranking, verification, privileged governance, or access to vulnerable groups. Funding models in [SUSTAINABILITY.md](SUSTAINABILITY.md) are proposals with dated estimates, not established income or eligibility. A solo-maintainer project cannot truthfully claim five independent funders or operational redundancy.

Before a recurring service promise, demonstrate that another consenting maintainer can rebuild the published site from a dated export and restore the prior version. If the primary maintainer stops, show an unmaintained notice and last review date; preserve a downloadable export under its actual source terms; offer a handoff. A static archive is more credible than an unattended site presenting stale records as current.

## Decision record, not another manifesto

At day 30 publish one page: task and locality, participants recruited/completed, baseline comparison, accepted/rejected leads, corrections returned upstream, agreed introductions, maintenance hours, observed harms, and continue/revise/stop decision. Link the result to the relevant claim and falsifier. If participants obtain equivalent value from a source directory, strengthening that source is a valid successful contribution.
