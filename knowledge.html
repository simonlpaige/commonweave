Framework knowledge base

How could people meet shared needs through cooperation, common ownership and democratic decisions?

The framework

Read the proposal at different levels of detail.

  • Framework overview

    The central question, principles, tensions and current state of the project.

  • Systems to build and maintain

    The blueprint for democratic infrastructure, material provision and economic change.

  • How change might happen

    Mechanisms of institutional change, political resistance and the risks of losing gains.

  • Historical context and open questions

    A longer companion to the proposal. Read its examples alongside the critique and source checks.

Ten areas of work

Start with a need or a shared resource. These topics also organize the map.

  • Healthcare

    How do communities support care, compensate workers and maintain public health systems?

  • Education

    What do open learning resources need from teachers, institutions and community governance?

  • Food sovereignty

    Explore food production, distribution, seed access and the limits of local self-sufficiency.

  • Democratic infrastructure

    Who takes part, how are decisions made, and what prevents concentrated power?

  • Land and housing

    Examine land trusts, housing provision and the constraints of land, finance and construction.

  • Ecological restoration

    How could ecological repair be sustained through investment, measurement and public decisions?

  • Conflict resolution

    Research leads on restorative justice, community accountability and responses to harm.

  • Cooperatives and solidarity

    Consider shared ownership, community wealth and the pressures of capital and competition.

  • Recreation and arts

    Research leads on creative work, cultural commons, public libraries and shared space.

  • Energy and digital commons

    Explore community energy and shared digital infrastructure, including ownership and upkeep.

Evidence and critique

Distinguish a proposal from evidence, and look for what would change the conclusion.

  • Critique of the framework

    A section-by-section account of weak arguments, missing evidence and unresolved tensions.

  • Claim ledger

    The framework's explicit claims, their support and their stated uncertainty.

  • What would prove us wrong?

    Observable tests that should lead to revision, narrower claims or a change of direction.

  • Competing approaches

    The strongest cases for alternative theories and what Commonweave could learn from them.

  • Public-data audit

    September 2026 structural checks of the shipped records, locations, links and evidence gaps.

  • Featured-organization source checks

    Eight dated reviews separating an organization's stated role from unsupported claims.

Practice and governance

Make a test small enough to learn from, and account for its upkeep and risks.

  • A testable operating model

    A proposed local pilot with a comparison baseline, responsibilities, time budget and stop rules.

  • Decisions and accountability

    Project roles, decision-making, challenges to decisions and unresolved governance problems.

  • Capture, abuse and failure

    Ways the project could be manipulated or cause harm, with possible countermeasures.

  • Keeping the project useful

    Funding proposals, infrastructure costs, continuity and an orderly wind-down.

  • Lessons from WiserEarth

    Notes on an earlier organization directory and the questions its closure raises.
