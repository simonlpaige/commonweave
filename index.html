Find organizations already working on a shared need.

Commonweave is an open directory of cooperatives, community land trusts, mutual aid networks and other community organizations. Pick something you want to do and a place. It lists organizations to investigate for each role, and shows the roles where it has no record.

How to use a result

  1. Name one task and one place

    “Find experience with shared growing space in our town” can be investigated. “Build a better food system” cannot.

  2. Look at each role

    A food project may need a grower, a site and people who understand member ownership. Leave missing roles visible.

  3. Check each lead at its source

    Read the organization’s own site. Confirm what it does now, who it serves and whether it welcomes your inquiry.

What it is good for

Finding names you did not know to look for, seeing which kinds of organization a project usually needs, and noticing which of those have no record where you are.

What it cannot do

Confirm that an organization is active, measure distance or service area, or show that two organizations work together. The three files were built at different times and do not match.

The framework behind it

A set of proposals about meeting shared needs through cooperation, common ownership and democratic decisions. They are hypotheses, published with their critique.

Read the framework

  • HealthcareCare, access and the people who provide it
  • EducationLearning, shared knowledge and opportunity
  • Food sovereigntyGrowing, distributing and accessing food
  • Democratic infrastructureMaking decisions and holding power accountable
  • Land and housingSecure homes and stewardship of land
  • Ecological restorationCaring for the systems that sustain life
  • Conflict resolutionAddressing harm and resolving disagreements
  • Cooperatives and solidarityShared ownership and mutual support
  • Recreation and artsTime, culture and creative participation
  • Energy and digital commonsShared infrastructure, from power to software
